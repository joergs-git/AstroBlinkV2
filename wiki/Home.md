# AstroBlink & AIsaac — Wiki

Welcome to the AstroBlink & AIsaac wiki — your complete guide to the fastest astrophotography triage tool on macOS.

## What is AstroBlink?

AstroBlink is a native macOS app for blinking through hundreds of FITS and XISF sub-exposures, finding the bad ones, and moving them out of the way — all without permanently deleting a single file. Built from the ground up for Apple Silicon with Metal GPU compute.

## What is AIsaac?

AIsaac is your built-in AI astrophotography assistant, named after **Isaac Newton** — astronomer, physicist, and inventor of the reflecting telescope. AIsaac understands your equipment, your session data, and every frame's quality metrics. Ask him anything, in any language.
