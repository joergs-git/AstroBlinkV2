AutoRotate — visual alignment across filters, exposures, and nights

Every frame of a target now pixel-locks to a single reference using the plate-solve data your capture software already writes (ASIAir, NINA, and others). Direct matrix math — microseconds per frame, filter-independent, exposure-independent, works across any camera rotator angle and pointing offset. Blink through a multi-night session and stars stay rigid; only tracking errors, focus drift, and trailing actually move. Smart reference selection picks the frame closest to the median pointing, so a mount that lost center on one frame can never drag the rest with it.

Quality Feedback (A key)

Tell the algorithm whether you agree with its quality tier. Press A to cycle Agree → Disagree → Partly → Clear. New "FB" column with colored icons right next to Q. Feedback is persisted per-setup and uploaded anonymously to the community baseline so detection thresholds can be tuned from real user agreement rates.

Smarter star chain detection at short focal lengths

The tracking-hop detector now scales with plate scale. Fixes false positives on dense wide-field targets like NGC 2024 at 468mm focal length without affecting long-FL setups. Behavior interpolates smoothly — no discontinuous jumps between adjacent focal lengths.
